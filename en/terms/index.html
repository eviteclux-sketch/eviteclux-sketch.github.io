1. Purpose

These terms govern the use of the Prix Net Réel website, published by EVITEC LUX SARL-S.

2. Free service

The detailed calculation, the comparison of your calculations, the PDF and the guides are free and require no sign-up. Your calculations are stored in your browser, on your device.

3. AI assistant

The AI assistant is free for 2 calculations per visitor, with no sign-up; the free calculations are limited per device and per connection. After that, it can be accessed by subscription, or with a personal access code provided by the publisher. The assistant asks the questions and has the engine do the calculations; its answers may contain errors: check every figure in the detailed calculation.

4. Subscription

The subscription costs €9 per month. It gives access to the AI assistant for the paid period, within the limit of 200 questions per month; the monthly processing volume is also capped to prevent abuse. It renews automatically every month. Payment is collected by Stripe when you subscribe and then at each renewal; if a payment fails, access is suspended.

After payment, the site shows you a personal access code: you use it to sign in on another device. A code must not be shared; the publisher may deactivate a code that is used abusively.

During the test phase, payment runs in Stripe's test mode: no real card is charged, and test subscriptions are limited to 20 questions per month.

5. Cancellation

You can stop the subscription whenever you like from “Manage my subscription”. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the month already paid; access remains open until then.

6. Results

The calculations are based on the data you enter or give to the assistant. You alone decide on your prices. The publisher cannot be held liable for any decision made on the basis of a simulation.
